| **B-01** | **S1** | `LadderBackend/supabase/functions/invite-redeem/index.ts:58` vs `supabase/migrations/0010,0013,0018:39` | Every issued invite code is rejected. Edge fn hashes lookup with **HMAC-SHA256(INVITE_HMAC_SECRET)**; DB stores `digest(v_code, 'sha256')` (plain). They will never match — and the "uniform_failure" response hides the bug. Fix: change `hmacCode()` to `crypto.subtle.digest('SHA-256', …)`, or migrate the columns to HMAC and backfill. | **NEW** |
